Recurring Payment Platform
Merchant system will connect to bKash Recurring Payment platform via APIs. When a user initiates a
subscription payment in their system, merchant will send request to bKash with the payment details.
bKash will then provide a secure payment page where the customer will give consent for the entire
subscription by providing and verifying their account information. Recurring Payment Platform will set
up and maintain the payment subscription, and at the agreed cycle time, deduct the agreed amount
from the customer’s bKash account.
Customer Interaction Flow
1. Select subscription at merchant website/app
2. Agree terms and conditions
4 | P a g e
3. Enter wallet number

4. Enter OTP

5 | P a g e
5. Enter PIN
6. Complete the customer validation steps and redirect to merchant site/APP

Subscription Creation Steps
1. A customer can sign up for a subscription in a merchant’s platform (Website/ App)
2. Customer will see subscription packages including following information in merchant’s platform
-
 Payment cycle
 Amount per payment cycle
 Start Date
 End date
Note:
- We are providing several payment cycles such as daily, 7 days, 15 days, 30 days, 90 days, 180
days, calendar month and calendar year. However, it will be the merchant who will determine
which cycle to use for a particular subscription and will only display those particular cycle
options.
- Start date can be same day of subscription signup. If merchant is willing to provide the service
right after the subscription, they can configure subscription sign up date as the start date of
payment.
- Merchant system will provide the end date of each payment subscription based on their
agreement with their customer. End date can start from the following day after start date and
be up to a maximum of 2 years from start date.
3. In order to subscribe for a service, customer will first choose a subscription
4. Next, merchant platform will initiate a subscription request to bKash via API
5. bKash secure payment page will open within merchant platform UI and bKash system will
redirect customer to a consent link.
6. Customer will be able to view the terms & condition of creating a subscription first.
7 | P a g e
7. After agreeing to terms & conditions, the secure payment
page will display the fixed criteria that the customer has agreed to the merchant page while
proceeding - Amount, Start Date, End Date, Cycle, Next Date, Next Amount and Total No. of
Payments.
8. Then one after one, customer will enter bKash wallet number, OTP and bKash PIN to give
consent for the entire subscription so that bKash can debit that particular customer account
during each cycle without needing repeated consent throughout the subscription period
Note:
bKash system checks validation for customer as per following:
- Registered bKash customer account
- Identity & account status ‘Active’
- No state tag is restricting debit transaction
9. bKash system will set up subscription in its own subscription engine
10. bKash will provide notification of subscription set up to both customer (through SMS) and
merchant (through webhook).
11. After successful subscription, customer will be redirected to merchant’s platform i.e. website/
App
12. bKash system will have a log of each subscription

Cycle payment:
Payment Flow:
1. bKash system will send a notification before payment date as per the following schedule.
Cycle Pre-Payment
Notification Day
Daily N/A
7 Days 1
15 Days 2
30 Days 3
90 Days 3
180 Days 3
Monthly 3
Yearly 3
2. Payment will be automatically initiated by bKash system at each cycle. For example, if
subscription was set up as monthly, with starting date of 5 June, then payment will automatically
be made on 5th of each month.
3. If payment is successful, both customer and merchant will be sent successful notifications.
4. If payment fails, customer will be notified of payment failure through SMS and merchant will
receive webhook notification.
5. In case of failed payment, bKash system will retry payment as per the following policy.
Cycle Pre-notification day No. of Retrial Retrial Period
Daily N/A N/A N/A
7 Days 1 1 2 Days After Pay Date

Yearly 3 2 Every 3 Days After Pay Date
 ** Merchant will get notification for each retrial attempt.
6. If payment still fails, merchant will need to manage the payment of that particular cycle with
customer directly.
7. bKash system will continue with regular subscription payment from the next cycle.

Subscription Cancellation
Customer Initiated
A. From Merchant’s Website/App:
1. If a customer is willing to cancel any of his/her existing subscription, s/he will be able to do so on the
merchant’s website/App
2. User will log into the merchant’s website/App, go the subscribed services and select ‘Cancel
Subscription’. This is an example of the most simplified process of cancellation for any subscription.
This process might vary from merchant to merchant
3. After receiving the cancelation request, merchant will forward the request to bKash immediately
4. Once received, bKash system will execute the request automatically.
12 | P a g e
5. After successful cancellation, both merchant and customer
will be notified B. From bKash Customer Service:
• If a customer is willing to cancel any of his/her existing subscription, and for some reason cannot get
it canceled through his/her merchant, s/he can do so by calling bKash customer care
• CS will validate the customer’s identity in their usual process
• Upon necessary validation, CS will search by customer account number in CIMT portal
• CIMT System will show all the subscription under that customer account, and will show the
subscription
ID, start date, end date, amount, frequency, status (active/ended/canceled)
CS will cancel subscription in CIMT

Webhook Notification
Notifications must be verified to make sure a notification is indeed from bKash and not from a malicious
source.
Here you have a signature, payload, and api_key and you want to verify the signature.
For example:
payload = '{"message":null,"timeStamp":"2021-04-
19T10:08:22.267540Z","subscriptionRequestId":"573a44e6-5686-465a868ce7571b0233b1","paymentId":73,"paymentStatus":"SUCCEEDED_PAYMENT","subscriptionId":40,"tr
xId": "8DJ9022ZD7","amount":51.00,"dueDate":"2021-04-19","nextPaymentDate":"2021-04-
26","trxDate":"2021-04-19T16:08:22","firstPayment":true}'; signature
= "VL5Qow6bpQQnhHIYzf35rs5nssCGRc-Hih6I61ZC8jY=" api_key =
"3EbBE-L30-PEDU-KjkhxpEop8SYsanOa"
Signature decryption Steps: Verify the payloads that bKash sends to your webhook endpoints,
following python examples can be applied:
• Decode the base64-encoded header X-Signature to get the signature signature =
base64.urlsafe_b64decode(signature)
• Decode the base64-encoded api_key to get the key.
key = base64.urlsafe_b64decode(api_key)
• Generate hmac digest (using sha256 hashing) of the payload using the key digest =
hmac.new(key=key, msg=payload, digestmod=hashlib.sha256).digest()
• Compare the digest data with signature (hmac.compare_digest(digest, signature)

can have the option to choose the Web hook or the SMS or both.
