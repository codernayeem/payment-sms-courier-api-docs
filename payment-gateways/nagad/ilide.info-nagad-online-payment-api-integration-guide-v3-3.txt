5.2.7 Purchase Status
Purchase Status defined the state a transaction is in. Operations allowed on a particular
transaction request depends on the state it is in. Some of the states are end state and some
of them are transient ones kept to maintain the life cycle of a transaction. Current Purchase
Statuses are:
1. Success
2. OrderInitiated
3. Ready
4. InProgress
5. OtpSent
6. OtpVerified
7. PinGiven
8. Cancelled
9. PartialCancelled
10. InvalidRequest
11. Fraud
12. Aborted
13. UnKnownFailed
14. Failed
